Connect an AI assistant to your Cardlake feed

Cardlake is a personal knowledge and reminder app that brings notes, learnings, bookmarks and work follow-ups into one daily feed. It has a built-in MCP server that lets Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or anything else that speaks MCP read and manage your cards directly. Ask it to add a reminder, check your feed, or tick off a checklist, and it can just do it.

How does connecting Cardlake work?

Add Cardlake's server address to a compatible assistant, authorize access to your Cardlake account, then ask the assistant to save or retrieve a card. Sign in with your Cardlake email and password in each assistant to reach the same feed. Connector availability depends on your assistant, plan and workspace settings.

The connection shares your saved Cardlake items, not your full chat history.

Using Claude Code, the Gemini CLI, or another tool instead?

These take a token instead of signing you in directly.

  1. Open your Cardlake vault, sign in, and click Connect AI next to your name.
  2. Give the token a name, such as “Claude Code”, and click Generate token.
  3. Copy it right away; it's shown only once. Keep it as secret as a password.
Claude Code
claude mcp add --transport http cardlake YOUR_CARDLAKE/api/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN_HERE"
Gemini CLI and others

Add an MCP server entry pointing at YOUR_CARDLAKE/api/mcp, with an Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN_HERE header. The exact settings screen varies by version.

Back in the Connect AI panel, you can see every token you've created and revoke any of them at any time. Whatever was using it stops working right away.
