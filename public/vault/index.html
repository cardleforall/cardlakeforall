About Cardlake

Cardlake is a private, flexible home for the signals that matter: ideas to learn, plans to move forward, and moments to keep close.

What this is about

Capture lightly, review deliberately, and take action with less context switching. Your feed brings learning, work, family, and reminders together without making them compete.

How to use this tool

  • Snooze a card to see it again later; Complete or Delete clears it. On a phone, swipe right or left.
  • Pin up to three cards to keep them on top, and heart a card to find it under Favorites.
  • Filter by type or search to focus the feed, and use Deal me 5 for fresh picks.
  • Connect AI lets Claude or ChatGPT save and find cards for you.

Get the app

Install Cardlake on your phone or computer to open your feed from the home screen, in its own window.

On iPhone or iPad, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
