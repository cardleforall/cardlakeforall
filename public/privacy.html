Privacy & data

What this Cardlake stores about you, where it lives, and who can see it.

This Cardlake is run privately by the person who set it up, its owner, on their own Vercel and Neon accounts, for themselves. It has one account, the owner's. It isn't a service run by a company, and nothing is sent to the people who make Cardlake.

Where your data lives

Everything is kept in the owner's own accounts: cards, accounts and history in a Postgres database on Neon, attached files in private Vercel Blob storage, and the app itself on Vercel. Nobody else runs a copy of it, and nothing is sent to the people who make Cardlake.

Your cards

The reminders, learnings, work items, family updates, bookmarks, Misc cards and files you add belong to the owner's account.

Deleting a card removes it everywhere you see it. The database keeps a deleted marker on the row rather than erasing it straight away.

Starter cards, and cards added by "Deal me 5", are removed after 14 days unless you like, pin or edit them or attach a file.

Signing in

The owner's account is a name, email address and password. Only a scrambled form of the password (a bcrypt hash) is stored, so it can't be read back, even from the database. The email address isn't checked: it is simply what you sign in with.

Cardlake sets a cookie to keep you signed in on each device. It lasts 30 days from the last time you used Cardlake there, and Sign out on all devices under About & help ends it everywhere. A short-lived cookie is also set while you approve an AI assistant's access. Display preferences are kept in your browser's storage and never leave it.

GitHub Issues

If you connect GitHub Issues, Cardlake stores your access token, encrypted before it is written to the database and never shown back to anyone, the repository's name, and a copy of its issues so they can appear in your feed. Disconnecting deletes the token and takes the issues out of your feed.

AI assistants

An assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT can reach your cards only after you connect it, and only your own. Tokens you create under Connect AI can be revoked there at any time.

When you ask a connected assistant to read or change your cards, the cards involved pass through that assistant's provider (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) to answer you. Their own privacy terms cover what happens on their side. Cardlake itself sends nothing to an AI provider, and doesn't use your cards to train anything.

Your activity history

Cardlake keeps a history of what you do in it, to order your daily feed. It stays in your own database: cards you create, edit or delete (which fields changed, not what you typed), what you do in the feed and which cards you open, searches and filters, signing in and out, and the tools an AI assistant uses for you. It never records the text of your cards or any password or token.

How long things are kept

A daily job deletes data once it's past these periods: deleted cards and their files after 90 days, and the activity history after 24 months. Cards you keep stay until you delete them.

Other services involved

  • Vercel and Neon, which host this Cardlake for its owner.
  • GitHub, contacted by the server only if you connect it.
  • Sites your cards link to: with media previews on, your browser loads a link's thumbnail straight from that site, which sees your IP address. The Previews control in the feed turns this off.
  • Whichever AI assistant you connect, only if you connect one.
