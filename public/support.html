Support

Something not working, or a question? The common snags and their fixes are below.

New here? Cardlake is a personal knowledge and reminder app: your notes, learnings, bookmarks and work follow-ups in one daily feed.

Who runs this

The person who set up this Cardlake runs it, for themselves, in their own Vercel and Neon accounts. There's no company behind it to write to. The privacy page says what's kept and for how long.

Signing in

"I forgot my password."Follow "How to reopen setup" on the setup page and set a new one with the same email. You keep all your cards.

"I lost a phone or laptop."Open About & help and choose Sign out on all devices.

"How do I sign in on a new device?"Use the same email and password. You stay signed in there afterwards.

Connecting Claude or ChatGPT

Follow the setup guide. You sign in to this Cardlake when the assistant asks, and it only sees your own cards.

"The tools look old or are missing."Disconnect and reconnect Cardlake in the assistant's connector settings.

"I want to cut an assistant off."Remove Cardlake from its connector settings, or revoke its token under Connect AI in the app.

Saving from your Android phone

Open this Cardlake in Chrome, then open the menu and choose Install app. Now you can share text or links from any app to Cardlake; tap Save card.

Photos and files can't be shared straight in yet. Add them in Cardlake with New card → File. Images, PDFs, text, Word and Excel files work, up to 4 MB each.
